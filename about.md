## Hi, I'm Rahul

I work in neural rendering, focused on light transport and materials. Currently a grad student at DigiPen Institute of Technology. Alongside that research, I build a custom C++ game engine on the side.

This site is a public notebook. I write about what I'm building, what I'm reading, what I'm thinking, in whatever order those things happen.

## What's here

**[Essays](/essays)** are the longer pieces: technical write-ups, recaps. Edited when they feel done enough.

**[Projects](/projects)** are things I've built. Mostly tools and research code.

**[Bookshelf](/bookshelf)** is what I'm reading or have read, with brief notes.

## A note on AI

Although AI has its advantages, it's pointless to have that on a blog meant to share my thoughts. All the words here are written by me, a human. There will be grammatical errors and typos, and I hope you kind people forgive me for it.

## How to reach me

Email is the best place to find me.

## Colophon

Built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted on GitHub Pages. Typeset in Archivo, with JetBrains Mono for code. Written in plaintext Markdown.
