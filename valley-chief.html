about the game

Chief of the Valley is a turn-based tech company management sim that puts you in the shoes of a startup founder at the birth of personal computing. Every decision matters: who you hire, what you build, which technologies you chase, and whether you can make payroll this week.

Start with nothing but personal savings and a reputation to build. Take on contracts to keep the lights on, assemble a team of engineers and designers, and inch your way up the ladder while navigating a market full of rivals with their own agendas.

Built in C++17 with raylib, with no engine shortcuts. Every system, from the UI to the turn simulation, tech tree, and NPC AI, is hand-rolled. The visual layer tracks the current era and swaps to a new theme when the first tech node of a new tier unlocks, giving aggressive researchers a visual upgrade before players who don't. An iOS port is currently in active development.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Chief of the Valley is developed and published by Rahul Nair (deadsoftie). This privacy policy covers the iOS and PC versions of the app.

Data We Collect

Chief of the Valley does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device. No account is required to play.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

The iOS version may use Apple's built-in crash reporting and analytics services (provided by Apple through App Store Connect) when you have opted in to share diagnostics with developers via your iOS settings. This data is anonymized and aggregated by Apple before being shared with us. We do not have access to any personally identifiable information through this service.

Third-Party Services

The game does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or social platforms. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Chief of the Valley does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Because we collect no personal data at all, the app is safe for users of all ages in this regard.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date.
